Operational teams should implement pre-dispatch customer verification, incentivize migration toward UPI (18.9% return rate) and CARD (14.7% return rate) payment methods, and establish localized return-management protocols in Tier-2 hubs to safeguard the cleaned total revenue of ₹97,358.30.
