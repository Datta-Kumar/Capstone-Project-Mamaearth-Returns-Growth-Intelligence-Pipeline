**Situation**

Mamaearth’s regional performance analysis establishes a verified cleaned total revenue of
₹97,358.30, revised from the raw total revenue baseline of ₹99,860.20. Following data
hygiene and reconciliation across the period, the true peak sales month was identified as
March 2026 (2026-03), which achieved the highest verified monthly volume at ₹20,318.90 in
revenue. Across verified payment channels, transactions processed via prepaid instruments
recorded baseline return rates of 14.7% for Card payments and 18.9% for UPI.

**Complication**

Data reconciliation revealed material reporting distortions alongside payment-channel
friction. The ledger recorded a duplicate reconciliation difference of ₹2,501.90 between
the raw and cleaned total revenue figures. This tracking variance notably impacted January
2026 (2026-01), which initially presented an apparent revenue of ₹29,582.10; correcting
for the outlier inflation lowered this figure to a corrected revenue of ₹11,637.10,
confirming that the month was not the true performance peak. Operationally, customer
returns present significant risk: orders placed via Cash on Delivery (COD) exhibited an
overall return rate of 44.4%. The concentration of this risk is sharpest in the segment
combining COD with Tier-2 cities, which reached the highest-risk return rate of 54.5%.

**Resolution**

Regional finance and operations leadership must adopt the cleaned total revenue baseline
of ₹97,358.30 and recognize March 2026 (₹20,318.90) as the true benchmark for demand
planning. Because the broader financial and logistical effects of product returns were not
directly measured in this dataset, leadership should initiate targeted investigations to
evaluate whether these elevated return rates impose operational friction, strain logistics
workflows, or create negative financial impacts. Immediate practical steps include
conducting a root-cause analysis on the 54.5% COD return rate in Tier-2 cities and
examining customer behavior across the broader 44.4% COD channel. Additionally, finance
teams should institutionalize automated duplicate detection to prevent future reporting
variances similar to the ₹2,501.90 reconciliation delta and the January 2026 outlier
distortion.
